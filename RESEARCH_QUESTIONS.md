# Future hypotheses — not tested

Eventual question: can book structure and order flow predict short-horizon midprice changes, and can any statistical predictability survive realistic implementation constraints?

- **H1:** Top-of-book imbalance predicts near-future midprice direction.
- **H2:** Multi-level imbalance adds information beyond level-1 imbalance.
- **H3:** Microprice deviation from the midprice predicts short-horizon movement.
- **H4:** Order-flow imbalance provides more information than static book imbalance.
- **H5:** Predictability decays as the horizon grows.
- **H6:** Much apparent predictability disappears after spread and execution costs.
- **H7:** Signals differ between high-activity and quiet periods.

Potential future horizons: 100 ms, 1 s, 5 s, 10 s and 60 s. Time precision, sample sufficiency, tie treatment and label definitions must be approved before evaluation. The existing microprice calculation checks units only. No H1-H7 evidence, IC, accuracy, AUC, model score, returns, PnL or Sharpe is produced in Phase 1.
