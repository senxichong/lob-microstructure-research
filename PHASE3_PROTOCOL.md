## Association and descriptive bins

Primary association is Spearman rho: Pearson correlation of average ranks, ties averaged after pairwise finite filtering. Pearson is secondary. Constant/insufficient samples yield unavailable, not a fabricated zero. Ordinary iid/asymptotic p-values are not computed or used.

For each feature, derive 20/40/60/80% cutoffs by linear empirical quantiles of its complete primary grid pairs. These are EX-POST DESCRIPTIVE BINS, not executable online thresholds. Assign Q1..Q5 with equality to the lower bin; do not split equal values by row/time. Duplicate cutoffs can leave empty bins, which remain explicitly empty rather than being relabeled. Record edges and all counts. Use these same primary edges for secondary horizon quintile diagnostics and bootstrap Q5-Q1 effects; intervals for those effects are conditional on the observed edges. Do not refit edges to improve monotonicity.

Report bin mean/median future return, mean USD movement, UP/FLAT/DOWN probabilities and signed direction edge. Monotonicity is Spearman(quintile index, bin mean return), with a second measure for bin direction edge; require all five bins nonempty for a five-bin monotonicity verdict. Positive monotonicity >=0.8 is the predeclared descriptive threshold for signed features. Q5-Q1 is an outcome difference, never a portfolio return.

Conditional feature distributions by direction include p05/p25/median/p75/p95 and IQR. Optional fixed OBI bins are predeclared as [-1,-.6),[-.6,-.2),[-.2,.2],(.2,.6],(.6,1] for OBI_L1/L5/L10/WOBI_L10. No other 149-feature search will be performed.

## Dependence-aware bootstrap

Primary block length 60 grid seconds; robustness lengths 30 and 300 seconds, all reported with no significance-based selection. For each length B, use 1,000 circular moving-block bootstrap replicates: sample ceil(N/B) starting positions uniformly with replacement from 0..N-1, concatenate B consecutive positions modulo N, truncate to N. Resample paired feature/target/calendar missingness together. RNG is NumPy default_rng(314159+B); the same draw schedule is shared across features. Within-block time ordering is preserved, including missing grid cells. This is never an iid row bootstrap.

Compute exact re-ranked Spearman in each resample. An equivalent multiplicity/weighted-midrank implementation may avoid repeatedly sorting duplicated rows; synthetic tests must compare it against explicit duplicated samples. Use percentile 2.5/97.5% bounds (linear quantiles), and also Q5-Q1 mean-return bounds using fixed descriptive bin edges. Record replicates, finite counts, seeds and all draw distributions. Circular wrap introduces an artificial end/start adjacency and stationarity is only an approximation for this single intraday session; 30/300s and time-of-day diagnostics expose sensitivity rather than certify coverage.

## Circular-shift null and multiple comparisons

Use 1,000 target circular shifts on the full 23,400-point clock axis, offsets uniformly sampled with replacement from [600,N-600] grid seconds, default_rng(271828). `shifted_y[g]=y[(g-offset) mod N]`; shift missingness with the target. No shift within 600 seconds of zero in either direction; no individual-row shuffling. Rerank finite pairs for each shifted series. Preserve the target sequence/autocorrelation modulo the circular seam and destroy the specific local alignment. Two-sided empirical p=(1+count(abs(null_rho)>=abs(observed_rho)))/(1000+1), so no zero p-values. Save offsets and null draws.

This is an approximate dependence-aware alignment null, not an exact exchangeability guarantee: intraday nonstationarity and persistent common state can affect its calibration. Report that caveat and time-of-day/placebo results. Apply Benjamini-Hochberg to the ten PRIMARY 1s empirical p-values; correlated tests need not satisfy its positive-dependence assumptions, so also report Benjamini-Yekutieli as the arbitrary-dependence sensitivity. Neither correction repairs misspecified marginal nulls. No event-level, secondary-horizon or control p-value joins this family, and no discovery is generalized beyond this session.

## Fixed robustness and controls

Only after the complete primary 1s analysis, calculate core Spearman/Pearson/quintile edges at 100ms/5s/10s. Five equal 78-minute session blocks A-E are [09:30,10:48),[10:48,12:06),[12:06,13:24),[13:24,14:42),[14:42,16:00) Eastern. Interior sensitivity excludes the first/last 30 minutes; full session remains primary. Ex-post activity and relative-spread terciles use linear empirical 1/3,2/3 edges and lower-bin equality, no tie splitting, on primary valid targets plus finite regime feature. Analyze seven directional features within these states; no trading filters.

Compute pairwise primary-feature Spearman using finite grid pairs. Full event-level primary-horizon correlations are HIGH-FREQUENCY DESCRIPTIVE ESTIMATES only, with no iid standard errors or p-values. Repeated book states, clustered events, overlapping rolling histories/targets and serial persistence mean 162,879 event rows are not independent observations; 1-second sampling reduces but does not eliminate dependence.

Generate exactly one N(0,1) control at every grid point with default_rng(161803), independent of all market data; never redraw based on results. Use the same 1s Spearman, all bootstrap lengths and 1,000-shift framework. A control alarm is empirical p<=.01 or a primary bootstrap CI excluding zero; investigate without changing seed or silently overriding a failure. Primary random-control pass requires p>.05 and primary CI containing zero; intermediate outcomes are explicitly inconclusive pending investigation.

Temporal placebo is the causal feature lag of exactly +600 seconds: compare feature(g-600s) against y(g), omit the first 600 points without circular wrapping. Compare to aligned rho on the SAME surviving pairs. Report every core feature and whether absolute association weakens, without trying other offsets. Naturally signed features must satisfy rho(-x,y)=-rho(x,y) within 1e-12. Controls are diagnostics, not proof that every confound is removed.

## Predeclared interpretation rules

Feature CONSISTENT EVIDENCE: expected positive rho for signed features (either sign for context), primary 95% CI excludes zero in observed direction, BH q<=.05, >=4/5 time blocks share its sign, same-mask 600s placebo absolute rho is lower; signed features additionally require five-bin mean-return monotonicity>=.8. WEAK EVIDENCE: q<=.05 but consistency criteria fail. NO EVIDENCE: q>.05 and CI includes zero. Otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. BY results and all block-length intervals remain visible even when they disagree. Family verdict maps consistent/weak/no/inconclusive evidence to the requested family language; a family needs at least one consistent member and correlated members are not independent replications.

Overall CLEAR WITHIN-DAY MICROSTRUCTURE ASSOCIATION requires consistent members with |rho|>=.10 in at least two of imbalance/microprice/flow families. MODEST requires at least two consistent directional families without that magnitude condition. WEAK / FRAGILE requires some adjusted primary evidence without those conditions. NO RELIABLE requires no adjusted evidence and all primary CIs including zero; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. These are descriptive rubric labels, not additional tests or claims of generalization.

H1: support if >=3/4 imbalance columns meet consistency criteria, partial if any positive adjusted evidence, otherwise not supported if all estimates nonpositive, else inconclusive. H2: support if microprice is consistent, partial if positive adjusted evidence only, not supported if nonpositive, otherwise inconclusive. H3: compare |OFI_1S| vs |OBI_L1| at 1s and |OFI_100MS| vs |OBI_L1| at 100ms: both greater supports, one greater partially supports, neither does not support; differences are descriptive, not a formal dominance test. H4: nonincreasing |rho| across 100ms/1s/5s/10s in >=5/7 signed features supports decay, >=1 partially supports, none does not support; changes of sign are explicit. H5 is inconclusive about statistical confidence because iid event uncertainty is deliberately not estimated; report magnitude/sample-size differences and dependence without conflating them with confidence.
